# How to add an effect and change its settings

This guide shows you how to put an effect on a track and change its
settings. The effect can be an EQ, a compressor, a reverb or a delay. For
the built-in effects, see [Devices](../reference/devices.md). For each
key of the panel, see [The device panel](../reference/device-panel.md).

## Add the effect

1. Put the cursor on the track in the arrange view or in the mixer.
2. Press `a`. A list of the devices opens: the built-in devices, and the
   plugins that paw found.
3. Type a part of the name, for example `Compressor`, and press `Enter`.
   paw adds the effect at the end of the chain of the track.

The built-in effects include `eq`, `compressor`, `reverb`, `delay`,
`chorus`, `saturation`, `gate` and `limiter`. A plugin, for example a
VST3 or CLAP compressor, is in the same list.

## Change a setting

1. Press `<Space>d` to show the device panel. It shows the devices of the track,
   each with its settings below its name.
2. Press `w` to go to the next device, and `b` to go to the previous
   device.
3. To find a setting by name, press `f`. Type a part of the name, for
   example `Threshold`, and press `Enter`.
4. Press `j` and `k` to go from setting to setting.
5. Press `+` and `-` to change the setting in small steps. A count gives
   more steps: `10+`.
6. To type a value, press `c`. Type the value, for example `4` for a ratio
   of 4:1, and press `Enter`. For a value below zero, type `=-24`.
7. To put a setting back at its default, press `x` on it.

## Use a preset

1. Go to the row of the device name with `b`.
2. Press `c`. A list of the presets of the device opens. Choose one and
   press `Enter`.
3. To step through the presets, press `]p` and `[p`.

To save your settings as a preset, press `<Space>w`.

## Compare and remove

- To hear the track without the effect, press `gcc` on the device. Press
  `gcc` again to hear the effect again.
- To remove the effect, press `x` on the row of the device name. Press `u`
  to get it back.

To open the window of a plugin, press `Enter` on the row of its name, or
`ge`.

In the graphical window, each device is a card in the device panel. Drag
a slider to change a setting.
