  async; Sonnet for mechanical migrations and splits; Haiku for read-only sweeps (doc searches,
  inventories).

  `cargo test --release` for touched crates, clippy (no new warnings), suite plugins, apidoc check,
  `nix flake check --keep-going`. Flaky under load: rerun alone.
