## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
**What to build.** In crates/paw-measure: an onset finder (spectral flux with an adaptive threshold, a sensitivity from 0 to 1) and a tempo estimate (from the onset intervals, with a confidence). In paw.api: `paw.api.measure_onsets(file or clip, opts)` gives the onset times in seconds of the source and their strength; `paw.api.measure_tempo` gives the tempo and the confidence. The work runs off the kernel thread, as the loudness measurement does, so a key never waits (rule 7).

**Core or extension.** Core, generic: it says nothing of warp markers or slices.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->
