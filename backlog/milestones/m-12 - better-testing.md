## Description

Tests that check behavior, are deterministic, and do not depend on time or load. Golden screens for the terminal. Property tests and fuzzing. A clear plan of what runs when (the tiers), and a flake policy with a quarantine list. Designs: backlog/docs/designs/test-suite-review.md and backlog/docs/designs/testing-practices.md.
